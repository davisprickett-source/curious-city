## High-Traffic Search Opportunities

Ideas specifically chosen because people actively search for these topics:

- "Is [city] a good place to live" - Honest assessments with data
- "[City] vs [City]" comparisons - Dallas vs Houston, Seattle vs Portland, etc.
- "[City] best kept secrets" - Hidden gems compilations
- "What happened to [closed business/venue]" - Lost and Loved expansions
- "Is the [urban legend] true?" - Myth-busting pieces
- "[City] crime rate truth" - Honest analysis without sensationalism
- "Why is [city] so [adjective]" - Explainers (expensive, cold, spread out, etc.)
- "[Historical event] anniversary" - Timely historical retrospectives
- "Best [specific food item] in [city]" - Ultra-specific food guides
- "[Neighborhood] gentrification" - Ground-level gentrification reporting

## Notes on Execution

**What makes these work:**
- Local knowledge that outsiders don't have
- Stories that make long-time residents say "I never knew that"
- Actual reporting, not aggregation
- Human subjects, not just facts
- Specific addresses, dates, names
- Archival images and documents
- Voices of people who were there

**What to avoid:**
- Generic "Top 10 Things to Do" content
- Wikipedia-level history summaries
- Puff pieces with no edge
- Tourism board talking points
- Anything ChatGPT could write without local sources
