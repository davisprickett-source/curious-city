# Title & Content Improvements — SEO & Engagement Updates

**Date:** January 19, 2026
**Goal:** Make titles more SEO-friendly, add city names where missing, maintain Morning Brew-style wit
**Style:** Punchy, engaging, witty, high literary nonfiction, bit irreverent

---

## Summary of Changes

### Key Improvements:
1. **Added city names** to titles lacking geographic specificity
2. **Made titles more actionable** (e.g., "How X Happened" vs. "X Happened")
3. **Emphasized numbers and concrete details** (602 deaths, 18 workers, 70 miles, etc.)
4. **Shortened subtitles** for better meta descriptions
5. **Made excerpts more punchy** with stronger hooks
6. **Improved listicle titles** for clarity and searchability

## Writing Improvements Made

### Stylistic Enhancements:
1. **More visceral imagery:** "creaking wooden stairs," "lynched from a lamppost," "exploded like gunpowder"
2. **Stronger verbs:** "razed" → "bulldozed," "arrived" → "stepped off," "said" → "promised"
3. **Active voice where possible:** "The city demolished it" vs. "It was demolished"
4. **Specific numbers:** Changed "over a century" to "142 years," "fragment" to "90-foot fragment"
5. **Punchy endings:** "Chicago has mostly forgotten it," "Denver didn't correct... until 2022"

### SEO Improvements:
1. **Geographic specificity:** Added city names to all titles lacking them
2. **Concrete numbers in titles:** "Killed 18," "602 People," "15 Minutes," "100,000+"
3. **Keyword optimization:** "Massacre," "Hijacking," "Disaster," "Capital," "Lost," "Forgotten"
4. **Action-oriented titles:** "How Minneapolis..." "When Fargo Was..."
5. **Year-specific where relevant:** "Denver's 1880..." "Raleigh's Lost NASCAR..."

### Engagement Improvements:
1. **Emotional hooks:** "daughter who thought he didn't love her," "marriages went to die"
2. **Irony and contrast:** "'fireproof' theater," "coal terminal that never came"
3. **Human details:** Named "Look Young," "3-year-old daughter," "mill workers"
4. **Surprising facts up front:** "no word for snow," "10-minute divorce," "flour dust like gunpowder"
