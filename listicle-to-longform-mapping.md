### Key Findings:

1. **Most listicle entries DO NOT have longform articles** - out of hundreds of listicle entries, only about 8-10 have corresponding longform articles

2. **Image inconsistency** - When longform articles exist, they typically use DIFFERENT images than the listicle versions:
   - Chicago Iroquois Theater: Listicle uses `/dark-history/` images, longform uses `/articles/` images
   - Fargo Divorce: Listicle uses `/curiosities/` image, longform uses `/articles/` image

3. **Path patterns**:
   - Listicle images typically in: `/[city]/dark-history/` or `/[city]/curiosities/`
   - Longform images typically in: `/[city]/articles/` or external URLs (Wikimedia, Unsplash)

4. **Article files that exist but may not have listicle counterparts**:

## RECOMMENDATIONS

1. **Standardize image paths**: Consider using the same images in both listicle and longform versions, or at least maintaining a consistent naming convention

2. **Add more longform articles**: Many compelling listicle entries (like Chicago's H.H. Holmes, Tylenol Murders, or Fargo's Billy Wolf case) would make excellent longform articles

3. **Create reverse mapping**: Some longform articles (Max Headroom, Boggs-Begich plane disappearance) don't appear in listicles and could be added

4. **Image audit**: Review all confirmed mappings to ensure images are appropriate for both contexts (listicle vs longform reading experience)
